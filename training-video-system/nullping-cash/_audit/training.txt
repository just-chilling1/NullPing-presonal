Pro tips

Publish before you polish

Your first money page does not need perfect copy. Activate, publish, and send traffic — refine themes and wording after you see Results.

Pins need a live destination

Always publish the money page first, then generate Pinterest pins so every pin points at a working URL.

Use your affiliate link

Add your affiliate URL on activate or in the money page editor so CTA buttons can credit you when someone buys.
