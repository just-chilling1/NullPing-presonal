NullPing Cash

Feature summary and known limitations

Prepared for management · 26 August 2026 · Activate. Publish. Get traffic.

1. Feature summary

NullPing Cash is affiliate software for beginners. A member picks a product, the app builds a hosted review-style money page, prepares Pinterest pin assets that send traffic to that page, and shows real visits and clicks in Results. Members still review, publish, and post by hand.

Core workflow

Step What the member does What the product does
1. Activate Asset Paste a product URL or name (optional affiliate link) Builds a full money page (headline, benefits, pros/cons, FAQs, CTAs)
2. Publish Preview, pick a theme/style, edit if needed Hosts the live page at /m/{slug}
3. Generate Traffic Request pin packs Creates 10 pins per run (image + title + description + tracking link)
4. Results Check activity Shows real visitors, affiliate CTA clicks, and per-asset stats

Supporting tools: Links Library (save/reuse affiliate URLs), Academy (tutorials + FAQ), Support, Account.

Premium upgrades

  1. Unlimited — ~200 ready-made money pages. Install with an affiliate link; each install includes 10 Pinterest pins.
  2. Done-For-You Profit — One run from link + niche → live page, 3 pins, an authority article, and 3 Facebook posts.
  3. Instant Income — ~10 Facebook post variants from a live money page. Copy/paste to Facebook; visits track with ?src=facebook.
  4. Automated Profits — Checklist of ~180 traffic sources across 9 niches. Guided manual posting, not auto-posting.
  5. Guaranteed High-Ticket Payouts — Add long-form authority sections to a money page from 100+ guides.
  6. Cyber Protection — Account health check (email confirmation, session, HTTPS, recent activity). Not antivirus.
  7. Reseller & License Rights — Request turnkey reseller activation; team unlocks after review.

2. Known limitations

What the app does not do

  • Does not auto-post to Pinterest or Facebook. Pins and posts are download / copy-paste.
  • Does not show commissions, conversions, or earnings. Those live on the merchant/affiliate network.
  • Does not provide a full analytics suite. Results are bot-filtered visits and CTA clicks (user-agent filtering), not enterprise tracking.
  • Automated Profits is a posting checklist, not set-and-forget autoposting.
  • Cyber Protection is account/session status, not security software.

Product and operations constraints

  • Pin generation cap: 5 runs per day (resets midnight UTC), 10 pins per run.
  • Pin images are template-composited (headline over a product photo or branded gradient), not AI illustrations.
  • If RAPIDAPI_KEY is missing, money pages and pins still generate using local fallback copy.
  • Reseller & License Rights is a request, not instant. Typical unlock is hours, up to ~48 hours when busy.
  • Scale Training exists in the codebase but is turned off in this product.

Training and launch gaps

  • In-app dashboard and Academy Vimeo IDs are empty. Members currently see “Training video coming soon” until IDs are added.
  • Production URL is not documented in launch notes yet (NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL must be set at deploy).
  • Price is not stored in the app repo (lives in the sales funnel).

Guarantee: 30-day refund, no questions asked (Support policy).
